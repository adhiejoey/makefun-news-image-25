One illustrated broadsheet summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026: **35 stories,
all first reported that day (UTC), across 6 regions** — space, markets, treaties,
quakes and more.

## How it works

`main.ts` copies the generated `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`).

The image itself is drawn by `gen.py` (Python 3 + Pillow, no network): a serif
masthead, a UTC timeline, three large illustrated leads, ten illustrated story
cards, a numbered ALSO list of 22 more stories, a dot world map with pins, and a
DATE CHECK box naming 16 excluded near-misses with their true dates.

## Stories

- **Leads:** Shenzhou-24 launches with the first foreign astronaut (OFFICIAL,
  Xinhua/Reuters); Asia stocks tumble on fresh tariff threats (CONFIRMED,
  Reuters); Australia–Solomons treaty signed (OFFICIAL, Reuters/pm.gov.au).
- **Date discipline:** every story was verified first-reported Wed 25 Nov 2026
  (UTC). Yesterday's top stories — the Zaporizhzhia strike, the Sanofi–Insmed
  deal, the Riyadh tower attack, Huang's apology, the Nepal quake, the CIA
  breach, the Huawei verdict — are named in the DATE CHECK box, not in the news.
